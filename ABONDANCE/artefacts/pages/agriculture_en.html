Line of reasoning

From photon to leaf

Nothing agricultural is understood without light: one einstein at 680 nm is 175.92 kJ/mol, and the photon count per CO2 fixed is a thermodynamic obligation, not a tuning knob.

From leaf to field: Monteith and Liebig

A crop's yield is ε·fAPAR·PAR as long as nothing is missing; the moment one factor is missing, that factor decides.

From nitrogen in the air to Bosch's ammonia

Air is free, fixing it is not: 31 GJ per tonne of NH3, hence 37.7 MJ per kg of nitrogen. That is the price of leaving seasonal hunger.

Water enters through physics: the two dossiers touch

Evapotranspiration returns the water dossier's litre, and the pumping kilowatt-hour comes from the same ρgH/η identity. No agricultural figure is typed by hand.

Soil and water as debts, not as costs

A 30 cm horizon wears at 10 t/ha/an while it forms twenty times slower: the farm is writing a debt, and the ledger shows it.

Sources and predecessors

The source video is about electricity: it supplies NO agricultural data — no yield, no photosynthesis value, no material is taken from it. Agriculture is our own extension, built with the same patron, and it stands on named predecessors: Thales and Anaximander, then Theophrastus on plants; Boussingault and Liebig for the law of the minimum; Blackman on limiting factors; Calvin on the carbon cycle; Haber and Bosch on ammonia; Monteith on radiation use efficiency; Allan on virtual water and Hoekstra on the water footprint; van 't Hoff and Pfeffer on osmosis. Thales, Anaximander and Theophrastus are lineage-only names here: none of the eighteen equations rests on them, and SOURCES_ET_PREDECESSEURS.md, at the root of this dossier, sorts every equation into computed law, declared value or lineage name.
