Line of reasoning

The measured source, and what it lacks

The starting point is a YouTube page measured byte for byte, cited here only as a bibliographic reference: its title « Le CERN a Enfin Détecté un Événement que la Science Attendait depuis 40 Ans » (Nocturne channel, id 5pP1e69T3HQ, declared length 8018 s). It serves only bootstrap JavaScript, its description carries no dated chapter and therefore no citable timestamp, and timedtext returns 0 bytes: the transcript is held to be absent. The dossier quotes no sentence of the video, reaches no instant of it and takes no number from it; it keeps one theme only, the rare decay K⁺ → π⁺ νν̄.

The axiom is set, and it proves nothing

EQ-01 states the One, set OUTSIDE THE MATRIX and attributed to M. Ahmed EL BAHLOUL (IMAM8MAHDI). The module carries `nombres_fournis_par_l_axiome = 0` on its face: no equation derives from the axiom, no CERN measurement confirms or refutes it, and the physics here follows neither a theology nor the reverse. What the axiom commits to is one checkable constraint: never declare the same quantity twice with two values.

Four pillars that are checks

Unity, Bound, Confrontation and Transmission are not decoration: each is carried by controls executed on every run, and `OUTILS/audit_piliers.py` applies them to the other modules of the deposit. A pillar becomes demanding when it costs sentences and can fail, not when it is announced.

The graph stops somewhere

EQ-02 and EQ-03 require that a node without an internal predecessor name a source declared outside the dossier, and that the graph stay acyclic. The chain of derivation therefore ends on a posited source, never on itself: that is the operative shape of the axiom, and it says nothing about the truth of the numbers.

A prediction frozen before the read

EQ-07 freezes the prediction on a dictionary of declared values and checks, by inspecting the function signature, that no comparator key can enter it; the contamination flag rises as soon as one is injected. EQ-10 can then set 10.0119 events against the published excess without having read the result in advance.

The confrontation: 2021, 2024, 2026 and the communiqué

EQ-08 to EQ-17 read four public sources. The dossier writes what agrees (the acceptance closes, the excess sits 0.7166 σ from the frozen prediction), what does not (the 2024 one-sigma intervals do not overlap under our quadrature hypothesis), and what closes again (the 2026 gap falls under 1 σ). None of these is offered as a discovery or as a refutation of NA62.

What SPECIFIED and SIMULATED mean here

Two equations carry SPECIFIED — the posited axiom and the written bound — and one carries SIMULATED: the geometric beam fraction, bounded from below because the communiqué gives neither useful time nor beam acceptance. These words appear on every page in all three languages, and no simulated result is promoted into a claim.

Writing one's own refutation

EQ-18 closes the dossier with its own collapse: five conditions that would invalidate it, four things it does not prove, and eighteen non-empty equation bounds. A finite computation does not found a universal assertion; it can only show that a discipline holds on public numbers. That is the whole claim of this dossier.
